# Habitos

A personal terminal habit game: real-life habits and one-off chores feed a game with depth and a growing pet.

## Language

**Habit**:
A recurring real-life activity the player commits to repeat on a schedule.
_Avoid_: Routine, daily

**Schedule**:
The rule that says when a Habit is due: every day, on fixed weekdays, or a number of times per week on any days.
_Avoid_: Frequency, recurrence

**Streak**:
The count of consecutive due periods a Habit was fulfilled, in its Schedule's unit: due days for daily and fixed-weekday Habits, weeks for times-per-week Habits.
_Avoid_: Chain, combo

**Freeze**:
A consumable earned in the game that covers one missed period of a Streak. It is spent automatically on a miss; a miss with none left breaks the Streak, which can still be repaired by spending a Freeze within 7 days of the miss.
_Avoid_: Shield, joker, streak saver

**Quest**:
A one-off real-life activity the player completes once and never repeats. It may carry an optional due date; finishing by it earns a bonus, missing it costs nothing.
_Avoid_: Task, to-do, errand, one-off habit

**Run**:
A week-long expedition through a map of Nodes, ending in a boss. It closes at the end of the week whether or not the boss was reached, and pays out Essence.
_Avoid_: Season, game, round

**Node**:
One stop on a Run's map: a fight, event, shop or rest. Playing one costs a Step.
_Avoid_: Room, level, encounter

**Step**:
The right to play one Node. One accrues per day, and only a few can be banked, so skipping a day can be caught up but a long absence cannot.
_Avoid_: Energy, turn, action

**Gold**:
The Run's currency, earned by completing Habits (scaled by Streak) and Quests (scaled by size). Spent inside the Run, including on Freezes; what is left converts to Essence when the Run closes.
_Avoid_: Coins, points, XP

**Essence**:
The persistent currency earned when a Run closes. It only buys Unlocks.
_Avoid_: Meta-currency, prestige points

**Unlock**:
A permanent addition to the pool a Run draws from: Node types, items, events, pet cosmetics or codex entries. Never raw power.
_Avoid_: Upgrade, perk
